# Data

- `vehicleParameters_2013ToyotaPriusFour.mat`: IVSG vehicle parameters for a 2013 Toyota Prius Four (used by the demo and tests).
- Put downloaded models (OBJ/STL plus .mtl) here, and record their source and license.
- Finished meshes are saved here as `CarMesh3D_<VehicleName>.mat` by the demo when `flag_saveToLibrary = 1`.
